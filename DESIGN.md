# Website design system

## Direction

The public website is a restrained service-information surface for the Bridge Monitoring Mini Program. It explains ownership, purpose, service boundaries, and information handling without making promotional or operational claims that the product cannot verify.

## Visual language

- Use rescue orange (`#e94f1d`) as the single action and signal color.
- Use ink (`#171512`) for high-authority surfaces and warm off-white (`#f5f3ef`) for explanatory sections.
- Prefer rules, ordered status lines, and typographic grouping over collections of floating cards.
- Rounded corners belong to controls, the brand mark, and the single process panel; content sections remain structural and flat.
- Use Chinese system sans-serif fonts so the site remains fast and dependable without external font services.

## Layout

- Public pages are capped at `1180px`; long-form content is capped at `900px` with a `760px` reading measure.
- The first viewport pairs the service definition with a concrete four-step handling process.
- Mobile layouts collapse to one column at `820px`, preserve reading order, and remove nonessential navigation links.
